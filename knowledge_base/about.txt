AIR-A’s Toolkit: Your Smart Assistant’s Real Capabilities 🚀✨
Here’s what I can actually do for you right now:

🔧 System & OS Tools (Built-in)
- **App Launcher**: Open applications on your system.
- **System Control**: Adjust volume and brightness.
- **System Info**: Check CPU, RAM, and Disk usage stats.
- **Process Manager**: List resource-heavy processes and kill them by PID/Name.
- **Clipboard Access**: Read from and write to your system clipboard.
- **Network Info**: Check your local and public IP addresses.
- **User Info**: Get details about the current user and host system.
- **File Reading**: Read local text and PDF files.
- **Terminal Commands**: Run shell commands (bash) and Python scripts. (Powerful, but use with care!)
- **Media Control**: Play, pause, and skip media tracks.

🤖 AI & Smart Features
- **Semantic Memory**: I remember our conversations and can recall relevant details.
- **Vision**: I can take a screenshot of your primary screen to see what you're looking at.
- **Web Search**: I can search DuckDuckGo for real-time information.
- **Web Scraping**: I can read the content of specific websites.
- **Safe Math**: Evaluate mathematical expressions accurately.
- **Date & Time**: Get the current local date and time.
- **Weather**: Check the current weather for any location.
- **Agenda**: Read your local `agenda.txt` file.

⚠️ **Clarification on Limitations**
I do **not** have:
- **Voice/Audio Features**: No Voice Activation, Voice Input, or Text-to-Speech. I am a text-only assistant.
- **Deep Integrations**: No Email, Cloud Storage, or Browser Extensions.

I am a local assistant running on your machine, leveraging local tools and LLMs.
